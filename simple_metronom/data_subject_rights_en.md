# Data Subject Rights
## Simple Metronome App (Huawei AppGallery)

**Effective date:** 26.02.2026
**Last updated:** 02.03.2026
**Request contact:** kamchatka_lab@mail.ru

## 1. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- **Request access** to your personal data we hold
- **Request correction** of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Request deletion** of your data
- **Request restriction** of data processing
- **Object** to processing of your data
- **Withdraw consent** (where processing is consent-based)
- **Request data portability** (receive your data in a structured format)
- **Lodge a complaint** with a competent data protection authority

## 2. How to submit a request

Send your data subject request to: **kamchatka_lab@mail.ru**

Please include in your email:
- Your full name (or identifier that allows us to process your request)
- Type of request (access / correction / deletion / restriction / objection / other)
- Relevant details (date, app version, device information, what specific data you refer to)
- Your contact email for our response

**Subject line:** "Data Subject Request — Simple Metronome"

## 3. Response timeline

We will respond to your request within a reasonable period, generally **within 30 calendar days**, unless a shorter legal timeline applies in your jurisdiction.

## 4. Identity verification

To protect your data security, we may request additional information to verify your identity before processing your request.

## 5. Request limitations

A request may be limited, delayed, or denied where permitted by law, for example:
- Repetitive or manifestly unfounded requests
- Inability to verify your identity
- Conflict with mandatory legal obligations
- Request would interfere with rights of others

In such cases, we will explain the reason for limitation or denial.

## 6. Important note: Mandatory advertising

The app displays mandatory advertisements through Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. Your data is processed for advertising purposes by default and **cannot be disabled**. This processing is disclosed in the Privacy Notice (section 1.2).

Data processed by Yandex Mobile Ads SDK is subject to their separate privacy policies:
- **Yandex Privacy Policy:** https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/
- **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK Documentation:** https://ads.yandex.com/helpcenter/en/dev/

Your data subject rights regarding Yandex's processing should be directed to Yandex according to their policies.

## 7. Complaints to data protection authorities

If you believe that processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority:

- **In Russia:** Федеральная служба по надзору в сфере связи, информационных технологий и массовых коммуникаций (Роскомнадзор) — https://rkn.gov.ru
- **In the European Union:** Contact the national data protection authority in your country
