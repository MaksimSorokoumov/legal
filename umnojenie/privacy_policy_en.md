This Notice explains how data is processed when you use **Multiplication Trainer** (package: `ru.kamchatka.trenajor_tablici_umnojeniya.huawei`) in Huawei AppGallery.

The app has no user accounts, no registration and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, phone number, e-mail address or contact list. The app is an educational tool for learning the multiplication table and works fully offline.

## 1. What data is processed

### 1.1 Data processed locally on your device
The app stores on your device (via SharedPreferences):
- learning progress, completed tables and session results;
- local learning statistics: number of answers, answer times, daily streak;
- unlocked modes (Super Marathon, Quick Math, Crossword);
- hint balance in the Crossword mode;
- app preferences: theme, sound and time-limit settings;
- technical service values, such as the timestamp of the last fullscreen ad.

This data is used exclusively for app functionality and is not transmitted to third parties. Learning statistics are stored only on your device.

### 1.2 Data that may be processed by the third-party ad network
The app uses the **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK** to display ads (banners, fullscreen ads after a session, and rewarded ads for unlocking modes and hints).
When ads are served, the third-party SDK may process:
- device advertising identifiers (e.g., OAID/AAID/GAID, where available);
- IP address and network parameters;
- technical device/app data;
- ad events (impressions, clicks, technical delivery events).

Purposes: ad delivery, frequency capping, anti-fraud, and ad performance analytics.

### 1.3 Data that may be processed by the third-party analytics SDK
The app uses the **Yandex AppMetrica SDK** for app usage statistics.
The SDK may process:
- device and app technical data (device model, OS version, app version and build, language, screen parameters);
- device identifiers used for statistics and app-install attribution;
- IP address and network parameters;
- anonymized app usage events and session statistics (screens opened, features used, app launches);
- technical error and failure reports.

Purposes: app usage statistics, audience and retention analytics, identification of technical errors and crashes, and app-install statistics.

### 1.4 Content you choose to share
The "Share" button passes only the link or text that you send yourself through the app you choose. The app does not send this content to the controller.

## 2. App permissions

The app declares network access (`INTERNET`) to load ads and to send technical data through third-party SDKs. The app does not request access to the microphone, files, photos, contacts, precise location or the device camera.

The store listing shows the complete list of permissions, including technical permissions declared by the third-party SDKs integrated into the app. Such permissions are used by those SDKs only for the purposes described in Sections 1.2 and 1.3.
