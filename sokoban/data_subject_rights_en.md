## "Sokoban: Box Puzzle" app (Huawei AppGallery)

**Effective date:** 22 Nov 2026
**Last updated:** 22 Nov 2026
**Contact for requests:** kamchatka_lab@mail.ru

## 1. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to:
- request access to their data;
- request rectification of inaccurate data;
- request erasure of data;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to processing;
- withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent);
- request data portability;
- lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

## 2. How to submit a request

Send an e-mail to: kamchatka_lab@mail.ru
Include:
- your full name (or another identifier allowing the request to be processed);
- the request type (access/rectification/erasure etc.);
- a description of the data and context (date, app version, device);
- an e-mail for the reply.

Subject: "Data subject request — Sokoban: Box Puzzle".

If the request concerns a child, it may be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

## 3. Response time

A reply is provided within a reasonable period, generally no later than 30 calendar days, unless a shorter period is required by applicable law.

## 4. Identity verification

To protect data, additional information may be required to confirm the requester's identity.

## 5. Limitations

In some cases a request may be refused or limited where the law allows (e.g. repetitive or manifestly unfounded requests, inability to identify the requester, or conflict with mandatory legal requirements).

## 6. Data processed by the app and by third-party SDKs

The app has no accounts and most data is stored only on the device, so most requests are resolved directly on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling the app stops further processing and deletes local data (progress, settings, level statistics).

Leaderboard records on the controller's server (chosen nickname, random identifier and key, results of levels and of the daily challenge, recovery code) are deleted on request: write to us with the nickname and the recovery code — we will remove the record. Anonymous level statistics are kept for 400 days and then deleted automatically.

Data processed by third-party SDKs is governed by the respective providers' policies:
- **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (advertising, non-personalized)**
  Yandex policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/
  SDK documentation: https://ads.yandex.com/helpcenter/en/dev/
- **Yandex AppMetrica SDK (usage statistics)**
  Yandex policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/
  SDK documentation: https://appmetrica.yandex.ru/docs/

Consent for ads and anonymous statistics is requested at the first launch and can be withdrawn by uninstalling the app. Device advertising identifiers can be limited or reset in Android/HarmonyOS system settings.

## 7. Complaints to data protection authorities

If you believe the processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. In Russia this is the Federal Service for Supervision of Communications, Information Technology and Mass Media (Roskomnadzor).
