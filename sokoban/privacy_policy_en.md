# Privacy Policy

Revision of November 1, 2026

This policy explains what data the game "Sokoban: Box Puzzle" (the "App") processes and why. Rights holder: Kamchatka Lab.

## What stays on your device

Settings (sound, vibration, appearance) and all game progress are stored on your device. We do not create accounts and do not ask for your name, phone number or email.

## Anonymous player in leaderboards

To show leaderboards, the App creates an anonymous player on our server: a random number and a random access key. They are not linked to your name, phone or email. The server stores: your chosen nickname, game results (stars, best solutions of levels and the daily challenge) and a recovery code that restores progress on a new device. The recovery code is shown in settings — write it down and keep it safe.

## Anonymous level statistics

At startup the App shows a consent dialog for ads and anonymous statistics. If you agree, it sends anonymized attempt results: level number, outcome, duration, number of moves, undos and restarts. We use them only to make the App better and to find and fix bugs. Results are kept for 400 days and then deleted. Without consent the App does not start.

## Advertising

The App shows ads from Yandex Ads (Yandex Mobile Ads) — a rewarded video you can watch voluntarily: it unlocks level batches, grants hints and an extra attempt in the daily challenge.

Ad display is covered by the consent you give at startup.

To serve and measure ads, the ad network may receive and process data about your device: advertising identifier, IP address, device model, system version, language, impression and click information. This data is processed by the ad service itself under its own privacy policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

Ads are shown without personalization and are not tailored to your interests. You can reset or disable the advertising identifier in Android system settings.

## Other data

The App does not use geolocation, camera, microphone, contacts or user files. Internet access is required for ads, leaderboards and progress synchronization. We do not buy or sell player data.

## Children

The App is not intended to collect children's personal data. If you are a parent and believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will help resolve it.

## Data deletion

Local data (progress and settings) is removed together with the App or by clearing its data in Android settings. To delete player data on the server (nickname, results, statistics), email us at kamchatka_lab@mail.ru with your recovery code — we will delete it within 30 days. Direct questions about ad network data processing to the ad service itself.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the current revision is always available in the App. Questions: kamchatka_lab@mail.ru
