# Data Subject Rights
## "Palettica: Palette & Gradient" app (RuStore, Huawei AppGallery)

**Effective date:** 28.10.2026
**Last updated:** 28.10.2026
**Contact for requests:** kamchatka_lab@mail.ru

## 1. User rights

Depending on applicable law, the user may have the right to:
- request access to their data;
- request rectification of inaccurate data;
- request erasure of data;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to processing;
- withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent);
- request data portability;
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

## 2. How to submit a request

Send an e-mail to: kamchatka_lab@mail.ru
Please include:
- your full name (or another identifier that allows the request to be processed);
- the type of request (access / rectification / erasure, etc.);
- a description of the data and context (date, app version, device);
- an e-mail address for the reply.

Subject line: "Data subject request — Palettica".

If the request concerns a child, it may be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

## 3. Response time

A response is provided within a reasonable time, normally no later than 30 calendar days, unless applicable law requires a shorter period.

## 4. Identity verification

To protect data, additional information may be required to confirm the requester's identity.

## 5. Limitations

In some cases a request may be refused or limited where the law permits (for example repetitive or manifestly unfounded requests, inability to identify the requester, or conflict with mandatory legal requirements).

## 6. Data processed by third-party SDKs

The app has no accounts and we do not store personal data on our own servers, so most requests are resolved on the device itself: clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app stops further processing and deletes local data (palettes, favorites, settings). Data processed by third-party SDKs and services is governed by the policies of the respective providers:
- **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (advertising)**
  Yandex policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/
  SDK documentation: https://ads.yandex.com/helpcenter/en/dev/
- **Yandex AppMetrica SDK (usage statistics)**
  Yandex policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/
  SDK documentation: https://appmetrica.yandex.ru/docs/
- **Google Fonts (font loading)**
  Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Device advertising identifiers can be limited or reset in the Android/HarmonyOS system settings.

## 7. Complaints to data protection authorities

If you believe that the processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Russia this is the Federal Service for Supervision of Communications, Information Technology and Mass Media (Roskomnadzor).
