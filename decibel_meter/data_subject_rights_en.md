## 2. On-Device Storage Model

The Application operates strictly without remote accounts or cloud databases:
- All audio processing is ephemeral in RAM.
- Saved decibel readings, timestamps, notes, and night monitoring reports are stored exclusively in your device's private SQLite sandbox.
- You can erase your records at any time from within the app interface (via History and Night Log screens) or clear all app data via Android Settings (`Settings -> Apps -> Decibel Meter -> Storage -> Clear Data`).
