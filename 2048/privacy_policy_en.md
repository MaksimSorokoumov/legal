# Privacy Policy
## 2048 Game (Huawei AppGallery)

- **Effective date:** 29 September 2026
- **Last updated:** 29 September 2026
- **Operator:** Maksim Sorokoumov (Kamchatka Lab), Russia
- **Contact:** kamchatka_lab@mail.ru

This Policy describes data processing in the Android version of **2048** distributed for Huawei AppGallery (Huawei package: `com.kamchatka.game2048.huawei`). The game has no registration, user accounts, or sign-in.

## 1. Data stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally, including mode statistics, results, achievements, daily rewards, coins and boosters, selected interface and sound settings. Your choice about personalized advertising is also stored on the device. These data support game functionality and are not sent to the operator's own server.

You can delete local data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Whether Android backup restores some data depends on your device and OS settings.

## 2. Yandex AppMetrica — analytics

The app uses the **Yandex AppMetrica SDK** for usage statistics and diagnostics. The game may send mode-selection events, achievement unlocks, ad-display events, and rewarded-ad completion events to AppMetrica. The SDK may also automatically process information about launches and sessions, technical app/device data, network data, identifiers available to the SDK, and crash information. The actual data depend on the SDK version, configuration, and availability of system identifiers.

AppMetrica is activated at app startup separately from the advertising SDK. The app has no separate switch to disable analytics. The first-run dialog gives notice that AppMetrica usage statistics are sent; the choice in that dialog controls consent to personalized advertising, not an AppMetrica setting. Accordingly, AppMetrica may process technical startup data before you answer the advertising prompt, including if you decline advertising. Analytics processing is carried out to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The project includes a Firebase stub. It does not connect to external Firebase or send events there.

## 3. Yandex Mobile Ads — advertising

The app uses the **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK** for banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertising. The SDK may process technical device and app data, network information, available advertising identifiers, and data about ad requests, impressions, and interactions. The exact data depend on the SDK and its configuration.

The advertising SDK is not initialized and no ad requests are made before you expressly consent to personalized advertising. The prompt blocks access to the game: if you decline, the app closes and asks again on the next launch. Your choice is stored on the device. You can withdraw consent using the “Personalized advertising” switch in Settings; after it is switched off, the app closes and asks again on the next launch.

This choice applies to the advertising SDK and does not by itself disable AppMetrica (see Section 2).

## 4. Third-party recipients and provider notices

Data may be sent to the relevant SDK provider while its service operates:

- **Yandex Ads / Yandex Mobile Ads:** [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/), [SDK documentation](https://ads.yandex.com/helpcenter/en/dev/).
- **Yandex AppMetrica:** [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/), [AppMetrica documentation](https://appmetrica.yandex.ru/docs/).

Providers describe their processing and retention in their own materials. The operator does not sell users' personal data. The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or contact list and does not include such information in its own gameplay events.

## 5. Retention and international processing

Gameplay progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it; Android backup may affect restoration of local data. Retention of data by AppMetrica and the ad network is determined by the respective providers.

Third-party services may process data in jurisdictions described in their materials. The precise processing location depends on each provider and its infrastructure; the operator does not claim that all data remain in one country.

## 6. Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request information about processing, correction or deletion, restriction of processing, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. The procedure and limitations are described in [Data Subject Rights](https://github.com/MaksimSorokoumov/legal/blob/main/2048/data_subject_rights_en.md).

For privacy requests, contact **kamchatka_lab@mail.ru** with the subject “Personal Data — 2048”. To exercise rights over data held by a third-party provider, you may need to contact that provider directly.

## 7. Children

The game does not create user accounts or ask children to provide contact details to the operator. If you believe a child's data is being processed in violation of applicable law, contact the operator at the address above.
