# Data Subject Rights
## 2048 Game (Huawei AppGallery)

- **Effective date:** 29 September 2026
- **Last updated:** 29 September 2026
- **Request contact:** kamchatka_lab@mail.ru

This document supplements the [2048 Privacy Policy](https://github.com/MaksimSorokoumov/legal/blob/main/2048/privacy_policy_en.md) and explains how to send a request to the operator. Your rights depend on the law applicable to your circumstances.

## 1. Rights that may apply

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- obtain confirmation of processing and information about your personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request erasure or restriction of processing where the legal conditions are met;
- object to processing in cases provided by law;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- receive data in a portable format where required by law;
- lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

## 2. How to contact the operator

Email **kamchatka_lab@mail.ru** with the subject “Data Subject Request — 2048”. Please include:

- that your request concerns the 2048 game;
- the right you wish to exercise and what action you are requesting;
- enough information to understand the request and, if necessary, verify it securely;
- a way for the operator to reply.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or excessive identity documents. If information is insufficient, the operator may request only additional information needed to verify the request and protect the data.

A parent or legal guardian may submit a request on behalf of a child. The operator may ask for proportionate confirmation of the representative's authority where needed to handle the request.

## 3. Response time and verification

The operator will handle requests within the time limits required by applicable law. If the law permits an extension, you will be informed of the extension and its reason. To avoid disclosing data to someone else, the operator may request proportionate identity verification; unnecessary additional data will not be requested.

## 4. Data on your device

Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally. You can delete them by clearing the app's data in Android or uninstalling the app. This does not automatically erase information already received or retained by a third-party SDK.

## 5. Advertising consent and third-party services

You can withdraw consent to personalized advertising in the game settings by switching off “Personalized advertising”. The game then closes and asks for a choice again on the next launch. This switch controls the advertising SDK and does not disable AppMetrica; the game has no separate analytics setting.

Data processed by **Yandex Mobile Ads** and **Yandex AppMetrica** is also subject to the providers' materials. Requests concerning advertising identifiers, ad events, or analytics records may need to be directed to Yandex:

- [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/)
- [AppMetrica terms and documents](https://yandex.com/legal/metrica_termsofuse/en/)
- [AppMetrica documentation](https://appmetrica.yandex.ru/docs/)

The operator does not maintain user accounts or store gameplay progress on its own server, and may be unable to link an individual SDK record to a particular person. The operator will nevertheless consider requests within the data and authority available to it.

## 6. Complaints to a supervisory authority

If you believe that processing violates applicable law, you may contact a competent data protection authority. In Russia, this is Roskomnadzor; for users in other countries, the competent authority is determined by local law.
